What we build for founders

Infrastructure to build on. A community to build with.

Most ideas stall for lack of the basics: a place to work, the right tools, and people who have done it before. We provide both sides so you can focus on the idea.

Infrastructure

Everything a young company needs to operate, ready on day one.

Workspace
Desks, meeting rooms and a pitch room for demos and investor calls.
Tools and tech
Software, cloud credits and help choosing how to build your product.
Company setup
Guidance on incorporation, ownership, contracts and finances.
Investor network
Introductions to angels and funds when your startup is ready to raise.

Community

People who push your idea forward and stay with you after launch.

Mentors
Founders and operators matched to your market who meet with you regularly.
Founder cohort
A group of teams building at the same time, sharing wins and problems.
Pitch nights
Regular sessions to practice your pitch and get honest feedback.
Alumni network
Ongoing access to the TDZ community after you finish.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a finished pitch to sign up?

No. The sign-up form walks you through the five parts of a strong pitch and shows how long it will take to say out loud. You can refine it before you present.

How do I become part of the TDZ team?

Sign up and send your pitch. Our team reviews every submission and invites selected founders to present their idea live. After the pitch session, we let you know whether you are joining and where you will start.

Can I start in the Incubator and move to the Accelerator?

Yes. When your team has a launched product and early users, you can move into the next Accelerator group with the same mentors and community.

Do I need a technical co-founder?

No. Many teams start without one. We help you plan how to build your first version and connect you with potential co-founders.

Does The Desert Zone take equity?

Terms, including any investment and equity, are shared with founders during the pitch session so you can review them before you commit.
